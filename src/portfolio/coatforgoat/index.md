# Coat for the Goat

### Submission for _ToJam 2026_

---

## Role

Lead Programmer, Game Design

## Engine

Unity 6

---

## About

Made in 48 hours by a team of two, Coat for the Goat is a resource management game where you play as a farmer gathering wool to craft a coat for your beloved goat. Feed, raise, and shear your sheep to collect wool. Things quickly take a dark turn when an infection spreads through the flock, forcing you to cull the infected sheep to survive.

![](Media/snowman_factory.gif) 

---

## My Contribution

For this jam I was mainly in charge of implementing the systems we had designed, this includes

* Sheep lifecycle
* Sheep interactions
     * Feeding
     * Shearing
     * Grabbing
* Culling tool
    * Allowing the user to create a N-gon, and checking for collisions inside the N-gon.
* Sheep logic and state machine
* Core gameplay loop
