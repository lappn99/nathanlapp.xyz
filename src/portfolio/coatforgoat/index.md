## About

Made in 48 hours by a team of two, _Coat for the Goat_ is a resource-management game where you play as a farmer gathering wool to craft a coat for your beloved goat. Feed, raise, and shear your sheep to collect wool. Things quickly take a dark turn when an infection spreads through the flock, forcing you to cull the infected sheep to survive.

![](Media/coatforgoat_gameplay.png)

## My contribution

For this jam I was mainly in charge of implementing the systems we had designed, including:

* Sheep lifecycle
* Sheep interactions
    * Feeding
    * Shearing
    * Grabbing
* Culling tool
    * Letting the player draw an N-gon and checking for collisions inside it
* Sheep logic and state machine
* Core gameplay loop
