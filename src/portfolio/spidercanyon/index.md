## About

_Spider Canyon_ is a fast-paced puzzle platformer that challenges players to navigate a perilous environment through precise control and mastery of dynamic movement. It was originally made for _Mini Jam 175: Gravity_, but after submitting it, my jam partner and I saw its potential and an opportunity to properly release a game on Steam.

![](Media/sc.gif)

## What I did

Since this project was a joint venture, I was the lead developer while my partner focused on art, level design, and testing. A lot of the programming effort went into refining the systems made for the jam and implementing new ones to make the game suitable for release:

* Refining the first-person controller
* Fixing bugs
* Load/save system
* Creating tools to aid level development
* UI and options menu
* Sound effects
* Visual effects

A big chunk of my work also went into the visuals of the game. The game underwent a *huge* visual overhaul between the itch.io release and the final release. This was probably the single biggest change, along with the first-person controller, and lots of tweaking went into both to get them _just right_.
