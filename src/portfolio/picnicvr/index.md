# DENG GAO SHANG GAO RēVERIE

--- 

## About

_DENG GAO SHANG GAO RēVERIE_ is an experimental VR experience for the _Meta Quest_ developed jointly between _Invalid Symbol_ and _![Music Picnic](http://www.musicpicnic.com/)_. Split into two parts, The user is transported to a surreal - but tranquil - land where they are required to not do anything but soak it all in; set to music of Toronto Composer _Njo Kong Kie_. The experience also features serveral *hidden* ways to interact with the environment via hand gestures. 

---

## Project Info

Project Type: Freelance

Time on Project: About 3 months

Engine Used: Unity

---

## What I did

### Hand gesture interactions

I was originally brought onto to design and implement the various _hand gestures_ with the `Meta Interaction SDK` that the player can perform during the experience. All in all around 6 of them made it into the final product. Not only did this mean the actual hand gesture detection but I also had to design and implement the environment reactions to the gestures. Typically this meant animating various properties of the `GameObjects` affected by the interaction, I also worked extensively with the Unity `Particle System`. 


#### Challenges

The biggest challenge was designing these interactions to fit the goal of the project and the desires of the client. I had to design the hand gestures and their interactions in such a way that they were extremely simple to perform but that the reaction from the environment was subtle enough as to not take away from the greater experience. These interactions were not what the end-user should be focusing on, rather, they are simply a fun easter egg the user could discover on accident (we didn't even allow the user to see their own hands in-headset).
