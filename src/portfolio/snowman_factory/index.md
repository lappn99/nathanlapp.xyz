## About

Made over 48 hours by a team of 4, _Snowman Factory_ is a chaotic resource-management game where the player builds snowmen to fill orders. Players construct snowmen by rolling snowballs around and attaching accessories to them based on the current order(s).

![](Media/snowman_factory.gif)

## My contribution

For this jam I was mainly in charge of implementing the systems we had designed, including:

* Player controller
* Snowball mechanic
* Snowman-building mechanic
* Order system
* Snowball accessory system and spawning

I also made the music, and sourced and implemented the sound effects.
