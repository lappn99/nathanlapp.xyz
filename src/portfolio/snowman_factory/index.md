# Snowman Factory

### Submission for _Micro Jam 52: Winter_

---

## Role

Lead Programmer, Game Design, Sound Design, and Music

## Engine

Unity 6

---

## About

Made over 48 Hours with a team of 4, _Snowman Factory_ is a chaotic resource management game where the user is tasked with building snowmen on an order based system. The user constructs snowmen by rolling around snowballs while attaching various accessories to it based on the current order(s). 

![](Media/snowman_factory.gif) 

---

## My Contribution

For this jam I was mainly in charge of implementing the systems we had designed, this includes

* Player Controller
* Snowball Mechanic
* Building Snowmen Mechanic
* Order System
* Snowball Accesory System and Spawning

I also made the music and procured and implemented the sound effects.
